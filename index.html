Chapter One

Once Upon a Hello

October 2021 · A friend's birthday dinner

It began with a seat that was not meant for either of us. A table of eleven, one chair short, and a conversation that started with an apology and did not stop for four hours. We talked about terrible films, good coffee, and the cities we still wanted to see.

Neither of us remembers who texted first. We both remember that nobody replied late, not once, not ever.

Little Moments, Big Memories

The years in between were made of small, ordinary, perfect things — airports and road trips, rainy balconies, burnt dinners, and festivals with far too much family.

Chapter Three

When We Knew

“It was not one grand moment. It was a thousand small ones, all pointing the same way.”

There was no single day. Only the slow, certain realisation that every good piece of news, every bad one, and every ordinary Tuesday was already being shared with the same person.

Chapter Four

The Proposal

December 2025 · Where it all started

The plan was dinner. The reality was a detour, a long walk, and a very badly hidden box. There were no fireworks and no crowd — only one question, asked twice because the first time came out as a whisper.

The answer, for the record, was immediate.

Chapter Five

Our Next Chapter

And now, the part we get to write with you in it.

We are getting married in the city where our families began, surrounded by the people who turned two separate stories into one. Come for the vows, stay for the food, the music, and the dancing that will almost certainly run late.
